1. Introduction

Welcome to MediBridge, a digital platform developed by VitalSync for the centralized monitoring and management of elderly care. These Terms and Conditions govern your use of our web and mobile applications. By accessing or using MediBridge, you agree to comply with these terms.

2. Ethical Commitment

VitalSync adheres to the ethical principles established by the ACM/IEEE Software Engineering Code of Ethics and the Code of Ethics of the Colegio de Ingenieros del Perú (CIP). Our development practices are guided by the following commitments:

  • Act consistently with the public interest, ensuring that our software serves the well-being of elderly patients and their families.
  • Maintain the highest standards of professional competence and integrity in the design, development, and maintenance of our platform.
  • Ensure that our products and modifications meet the highest professional standards, prioritizing patient safety and data confidentiality.
  • Promote an inclusive and respectful environment, ensuring that our platform is accessible to users with varying levels of digital literacy.

3. Data Privacy and Protection

MediBridge handles sensitive clinical data including vital signs, medical records, medication logs, and personal information. We implement the following safeguards:

  • JWT-based authentication with role-based access control (RBAC) to restrict data access by user role.
  • Encrypted data storage and transmission using industry-standard protocols.
  • Audit logging for all critical operations including medication administration and health record modifications.
  • Compliance with Peruvian data protection regulations (Ley N° 29733, Ley de Protección de Datos Personales).

4. Acceptable Use

Users agree to use MediBridge solely for its intended purpose of elderly care monitoring and communication. Prohibited activities include unauthorized access attempts, data manipulation, and use of the platform for any purpose that could harm patients or compromise care quality.

5. Limitation of Liability

MediBridge is a monitoring and communication tool that supports, but does not replace, professional medical judgment. VitalSync is not liable for clinical decisions made solely based on platform data. Users should always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical decisions.

6. Modifications

VitalSync reserves the right to modify these terms at any time. Users will be notified of significant changes through the platform. Continued use of MediBridge after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

7. Contact

For questions or concerns regarding these terms, please contact VitalSync at support@vitalsync.pe.
