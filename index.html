The Problem We Solve

Fragmented communication and manual records put elderly patients at risk every day.

Critical Information Gap

78.4% of nursing homes in Lima operate with manual records or non-integrated systems. Families depend on phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and in-person visits to learn about their loved one's health — creating chronic uncertainty and delayed emergency response.

Operational Overload

Caregivers spend 2-3 hours daily on manual record-keeping, reducing direct patient care time. 35% of preventable medical errors in long-term care facilities originate from communication failures during shift changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MediBridge.

How does MediBridge protect patient health data?
MediBridge uses JWT-based authentication, role-based access control (RBAC), and encrypted data storage. All health information is handled under strict confidentiality, integrity, and availability principles aligned with healthcare data regulations.
Do caregivers need technical skills to use the platform?
No. MediBridge is designed for simplicity — frequent tasks like medication logging require a maximum of 3 taps. We also provide on-site and virtual training for care facility staff during onboarding.
Can family members access the platform from abroad?
Yes. MediBridge is a cloud-based platform accessible from any device with internet connection. Families can monitor their loved one's health from anywhere in the world, in real time.
What happens if the internet goes down at the facility?
MediBridge is designed with graceful degradation. Critical data is cached locally and synced automatically when connectivity is restored. No vital information is lost during outages.
Is there a free trial for nursing homes?
Yes. We offer free pilot programs for nursing homes in Lima Metropolitana. Contact us to schedule a demo and start a 30-day trial with full Premium features at no cost.
